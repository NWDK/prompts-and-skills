# Decisions: the logo swap test

## The name was arguing with the piece

Drafted as "AI Design Slop Audit". The thesis is that whether a machine
made it is the wrong question, so the name contradicted the argument in
its first three words. Named for the test instead: the line people repeat
back to me, and it claims nothing about any tool.

## The signal register stayed home

The internal version carries a dated table of current defaults plus
comparison plates: the operational half, and the half that rots. A stale
tell-list flags things that stopped being signals. What's published here
is drift-proof by construction.

## The numeric score was built, then killed

A score out of twenty launders judgement into something that looks
measured, and survives long after the reasoning is gone. Four categorical
verdicts instead, including **Unscored**, for "I couldn't see enough."

## Genre controls are what make it an audit

Without them this is a banned-motif list with extra steps: punishing
editorial for serifs and developer tools for being dark.

## Blind-first is a real gate

Once you know a model produced something, you'll find its tells and can't
separate that from having seen them. It comes first or not at all.

## Two cases, called two cases

The own-family blind spot is reproduced twice, in both directions, and
described that way: not a law or a percentage. Escalating to a second
model or a person is worth it only when the call matters, is disputed, or
a likely-own-family verdict came back Clean.
