# WHY THIS EXISTS
# Whisper mis-hears names it has never encountered. Left alone it will turn a
# product name into the nearest ordinary English words — two real misses from a
# product walkthrough: "Smooth Matte" became "smooth Mac", "dual price" became
# "jewel price". Listing the right spellings here primes it before it guesses.
#
# TWO RULES
# 1. Only add spellings you are SURE of. A wrong spelling here actively biases
#    the transcript toward the wrong spelling, which is worse than no glossary.
# 2. Whisper weights roughly the LAST 200 WORDS of the primer most heavily, and
#    _global.txt is prepended to this file. So put the terms most likely to be
#    spoken at the BOTTOM, and keep the whole thing tight.
#
# Lines starting with # are ignored. One term or short phrase per line.
# Harvest as you go: any name you have to correct by hand belongs here after.
