# A Working Agreement With an AI Agent

Twelve clauses you paste into your assistant's standing instructions. They
tell it to say when it doesn't know something, push back instead of
agreeing, and ask before it builds.

Nothing to install.

## Use it

Copy the block in [`SHORT-VERSION.md`](SHORT-VERSION.md) into your
assistant's custom instructions, project instructions, or system prompt.
Replace the "My style" line with your own preferences.

It's 1,436 characters, sized to fit the smaller settings fields. If yours
won't take it, `SHORT-VERSION.md` says what to cut.

## Why

Every assistant I've used defaults to agreeable. You get confident guesses
instead of "I don't know", approval of plans with holes in them, and
finished drafts built on a question it never asked you. Writing down that
the uncomfortable behaviours are the job worked better than better
prompting did.

These are the standing instructions my own setup runs on, rewritten to make
sense outside it.

## Worth knowing

It only works if you enforce it. Point at the clause when something gets
through.

Clause six tells it to challenge you, so sometimes it'll challenge you and
be wrong.

All my accounts already run these rules, so I've never tested the cold
paste. It works for me.

## The two files

[`SHORT-VERSION.md`](SHORT-VERSION.md) is what you paste.
[`AGREEMENT.md`](AGREEMENT.md) is the same twelve clauses with the reason
for each one next to it, worth reading when a clause gets broken and you're
deciding whether to keep it. [`DECISIONS.md`](DECISIONS.md) is what I chose
and rejected building it.

## Feedback

Take it, change it, ignore what doesn't fit. If a clause misfires or misses
something you keep hitting, [open an issue](../../issues).
